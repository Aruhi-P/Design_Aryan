Your privacy

FitNotebook saves activity plans, completed sessions and habits in Supabase using an anonymous user ID. A browser backup is also stored on this device. No name or email is required. Data is transferred to the Supabase cloud, and anonymous sessions may be lost if you clear your browser data. Avoid entering sensitive personal details.
